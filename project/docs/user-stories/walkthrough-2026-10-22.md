## Findings

| ID | Story | Severity | Finding | Suggested next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SF-1 | SP-03 | High | A second download of an event already imported commits as a new event. It has the same name, term and date, and 35 of the same 38 people. Preview and commit show no warning ("35 rows imported", "Duplicates in event 0"). The Dataset page then lists two "Bagel Breakfast, Oct…" rows ([screenshot](screenshots/SP-03-reexport-dataset.png)). Chat then says the October breakfast had 73 attendees and calls it the biggest event this term, when it had 38 and the Health Careers Mixer had 45 ([screenshot](screenshots/SP-03-reexport-chat.png)). A committed import cannot be removed, so staff cannot undo it. `known-limitations.md` lists "One file per event" and "Committed imports cannot be edited or deleted", but not this effect on answers. | Design, then build: a preview warning when an event with the same name and term exists, or when most of the file's people are already in an event; and deleting a committed import. Ask the sponsor how often an export is downloaded again. |
| SF-2 | SP-09 | Medium | Event date is optional at upload. 12twenty files have no date, and the compiled sheet has none, so the samples on main have none. A date-range question leaves undated events out, and says so only in "How I read your question". On main, "between October 1 and October 31, 2026" answers 0. | Ask the sponsor whether date ranges matter or terms are enough. If they matter, require the date at upload (or warn when it is blank), and have the answer say how many events had no date. The second change touches the prompt, so re-run the benchmark. |
| SF-3 | SP-02 | Low (data on main only) | Main's four 12twenty events have a blank attendance-source note. `sample_data/events.json` now sets "12twenty dummy attendance export", and a fresh load (A1, and the sponsor stack) has it. Main was loaded before that change. | Before the sponsor demo, set the note on those four events in the dev DB (one `UPDATE`), or reload main from scratch. Not done here, because main was walked read-only. |
| SF-4 | SP-12 | Medium (depends on D1) | With "Let AI see the results" off, or with a model that may not see results, the summary is the local one: "unique_people: 79", "45 rows". If the sponsor does not accept result sharing (D1), every answer reads like that. | Confirm D1 with the sponsor. If sharing stays off, build a local sentence from the reading and the value, for example "79: the number of distinct people who attended at least one event in Fall 2026." |
| SF-5 | SP-07 | Low | Two readings guessed a student's gender from the name: "the number of her attendance records" and "his attendance records". | Tell the SQL-generation prompt to refer to people by name, then re-run the benchmark. |
| SF-6 | SP-12 | Low | One AI summary said the nine Engineering attendees each had "a School of Engineering email address". Their emails are plain `@uci.edu`, and the table has no such fact. The caution line "AI-generated summary. Check it against the table." covers it. | None now. Keep it in mind if the sponsor relies on summaries without the table. |
| SF-7 | SP-13 | Discussion | Service gaps can only be measured among people who came. The Hub has no roster of all graduate students and postdocs, so a school or department that no one came from never appears. | Ask the sponsor whether a roster or headcount by school is available. That would be a new data source, out of the MVP's scope. |
