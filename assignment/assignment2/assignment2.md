Reducing the staff time to reconcile event registration and attendance data from 20-30 mins to X mins will directly support the Graduate Division’s mission of serving graduate students and postdocs through events and professional development programs. By automating exact NETID and email matching and sending only uncertain matches for staff to confirm, the system will cut down repetitive manual work. This not only frees up an estimated X staff hours each year for programming and student outreach but also saves the department cost in the number of student employees they hire to reconcile the data. 

The MOV also advances the department’s vision of reliable, centralized participant data. With a clean, reconciled record, staff members can pull information such as unique attendees by school, events attended per student per academic year, and exportable lists for outreach to past attendees. This allows them to have greater insights in their data to forecast future event attendance and report the results to their management. Because staff can still confirm every uncertain match, the department gains efficiency without giving up control over identity decisions or raising FERPA concerns.
