Course Information
Class Structure
Each lecture period will consist of about an hour of lecture that includes hands-on group activities with your capstone project team. If you cannot make it to class, please contact me ASAP to make arrangements for you to complete the in-class assignments. Otherwise, unexcused absences will result in losing points from your overall course grade.
The remainder of each lecture period will consist of team meetings with the professor and TA.
If entire teams are absent, they will lose points on their weekly group assignment.
You will have weekly homework assignments to complete as a team.
 

Technological Requirements/Preferences

Catalogue Description
Focuses on designing and implementing a novel software system. Students work on their project while emphasizing communication skills in business and technical settings. Students practice presenting project ideas, developing portfolios.

 

Overall Course Goals
Software engineering is a team effort. Planning projects and coordinating the members of the team are critical activities to ensure the success of the projects. This course will teach you the non-technical skills necessary for effective and efficient project execution, going hand-in-hand with your capstone project. We will explore the topics at several scales, from an organizational perspective to a team perspective to an individual perspective. Understanding project management and other “soft skills” such as communication, conflict management, leadership, and ethics will prepare you for leadership and management positions as part of a logical progression in your career.

 

Learning Objectives
By the end of the course, students should be able to:

Describe the fundamentals of project management, including its methodologies, processes, and infrastructure
Successfully follow an agile software development process to complete their capstone project
Develop a business case
Develop, review, reflect upon, and continuously improve a detailed project plan, which includes:
Measurable organizational value
Project charter
Work breakdown structure
Schedule
Cost
Identify best practices for project management soft skills such as:
Teamwork
Communication
Time management
Leadership
Ethics
Conflict management
 

Grades
Grades are based on an overall average for each student that is computed at the end of the quarter. The overall average is a weighted average of the following (final grades and assignment grades may be adjusted based on the results of peer evaluations. Unless everyone on your team contributes absolutely equally throughout the semester, in the end you will not all receive the same scores on the team assignments):

Group Assignments: 70%
In-Class/Pre-Class Exercises/Participation: 30%

Academic Dishonesty
Learning, research, and scholarship depend upon an environment of academic integrity and honesty. This environment can be maintained only when all participants recognize the importance of upholding the highest ethical standards. All student work, including quizzes, exams, reports, and papers must be the work of the individual receiving credit. Academic dishonesty includes, for example, cheating on examinations or any assignment, plagiarism of any kind (including improper citation of sources), having someone else take an examination or complete an assignment for you (or doing this for someone else), or any activity in which you represent someone else’s work as your own. Violations of academic integrity will be referred to the Office of Academic Integrity and Student Conduct. The impact on your grade will be a 0 on that particular assignment. Please familiarize yourself with UCI’s Academic Integrity Policy (https://aisc.uci.edu/policies/academic-integrity/index.php) and speak to your instructor if you have any questions about what is and is not allowed in this course.
